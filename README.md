TIDEKEEPER is an original ocean arena-survival game: swim the Tidekeeper through 10 waves of the Ink Tide, auto-firing coral bolts, collecting pearls to level up and draft upgrades from a pool of 12, and facing the Ink Leviathan at wave 5 and the final boss The Maw at wave 10. Three difficulties, synthesized WebAudio sound and music, particles, screen shake, pause, best-score saving, and a full win/lose loop — plain HTML5 canvas, no downloads, no network.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser.

## How to play

- **Move:** WASD or arrow keys.
- **Attack:** coral bolts fire automatically at the nearest enemy.
- **Space:** Tidal Burst — a damaging shockwave around you (12 s cooldown).
- **Level up:** collect pearls dropped by banished enemies, then pick 1 of 3 upgrade cards (press 1, 2, 3 or click).
- **Win:** survive all 10 waves and banish The Maw. **Lose:** your health reaches 0.
- **P / Esc:** pause. **M:** mute. **R:** restart a run.
